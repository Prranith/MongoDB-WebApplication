Privacy should be understandable.

This policy explains, in plain language, what CodeLeaf may process when you use its coding workspace, database playground, assessments, and meetings.

Information you provide

  • Account details: when accounts are enabled, CodeLeaf may process your name, email address, college or institution, password hash, profile details, and verification information.
  • Workspace content: files, queries, datasets, code, and other content you deliberately save or send through the application.
  • Assessment and meeting data: room settings, question papers, submissions, participant information, meeting events, and moderation records needed to provide those features.
  • Support messages: information you choose to include when you report a problem or contact the developer.

Information processed automatically

CodeLeaf may process browser and request information such as IP address, timestamps, device or browser metadata, authentication events, execution diagnostics, and security or audit events. This supports rate limiting, abuse prevention, reliability, troubleshooting, and account security.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, save requested workspace or assessment state, execute bounded practice requests, run exams and meetings, protect the service, respond to support requests, and understand reliability. CodeLeaf does not need your private production records to provide its learning tools; use sample or anonymized data instead.

Storage and service providers

Depending on the feature, data may be stored or processed by the services configured for the application, including MongoDB for account data and Redis-backed services for short-lived rooms, submissions, and runtime state. Code execution and AI assistance may use external execution or model providers when you explicitly use those features. Do not include secrets or confidential data in requests.

Exam and room records may have service-specific expiration periods. A room is not a permanent archive. If you need a copy of your own work, save it locally before closing the session.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

CodeLeaf uses essential cookies or similar browser storage for authentication, preferences, and session continuity. The site may also use analytics or Google advertising technologies. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads according to their own policies and your available consent choices. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in or other features from working.

Your choices and requests

You can avoid sending personal or confidential information, stop using a feature, clear local workspace data, or request account assistance through the Contact page. Account deletion requests are handled through the application or support channel when the account system is enabled. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the service, providers, or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows when the current version was published. Continued use after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the new version.
