Use CodeLeaf for learning and responsible collaboration.

These terms describe the basic rules for using the CodeLeaf coding workspace, database playground, assessment tools, and meeting features.

What CodeLeaf provides

CodeLeaf provides browser-based tools for writing and reviewing code, practicing SQL and MongoDB queries, creating or taking assessments, and collaborating in meetings. Features may change, be limited, or become unavailable as the project is maintained.

Your responsibilities

  • Use the service lawfully and only with data and accounts you are authorized to use.
  • Keep your credentials and room codes private. You are responsible for activity under your account.
  • Use sample or anonymized data. Do not upload passwords, access tokens, customer records, malware, or other confidential or harmful material.
  • Do not interfere with execution limits, rate limits, authentication, proctoring, or another user's workspace.
  • Do not use CodeLeaf to impersonate someone, cheat in an assessment, submit work for another person, or facilitate unauthorized access.
  • Respect other participants during meetings and assessments, including the room's moderation and copy/paste rules.

Practice results are not production guarantees

CodeLeaf's database and code execution features are bounded educational sandboxes. Output may differ from a particular local compiler, MySQL release, MongoDB release, operating system, or production configuration. Review and test important work in the environment where it will actually run.

Content and ownership

You keep responsibility for the code, queries, datasets, and messages you submit. You grant CodeLeaf only the permissions reasonably needed to store, execute, display, moderate, and transmit that content for the feature you requested. You must have the rights to submit content and must not use the service to copy or distribute material unlawfully.

Availability and changes

The service is provided on an evolving basis. CodeLeaf may rate-limit, suspend, or remove access when needed for security, maintenance, abuse prevention, or legal compliance. No service can guarantee uninterrupted availability or permanent storage of practice state.

Questions

If you have a question about these terms or need to report misuse, use the Contact page. For data handling, read the Privacy Policy.
